### Project manager who builds the AI that does the boring parts.

I run client projects for a living. Then I got tired of doing the repetitive stuff by hand, so I started building agents and automations to do it for me. Now I do both: deliver the work and build the systems behind it.

## 🚀 What I do

* **Project delivery.** I own projects from the first discovery call to final handoff, for clients across the US, UK and Europe.
* **AI agents and automation.** I build agents and workflows that take hours of manual work off a team's plate.
* **Partnerships and growth.** I have run influencer and affiliate partnerships, email automation and PR campaigns.
* **Teams.** I built and led a 120+ member marketing team, with the hiring, training and processes that came with it.

## 🛠️ What I build

* **A scope creep agent.** It checks incoming client requests against the signed scope of work and flags anything outside it before it becomes free work.
* **Reporting and dashboard automations.** Status updates and reports that build themselves.
* **Email automation.** Segmented flows in Klaviyo that drove real revenue with no paid spend.
* **Vibe coded agents and apps.** Quick builds to test an idea before anyone writes a spec.

More projects will land here as I make them public. Check my pinned repos for the latest.
